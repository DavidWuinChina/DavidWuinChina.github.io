Misunderstanding Association Prediction Model for Mathematical Multiple Choice Questions (Kaggle Silver Medal) (2024)

Led the development of a natural language processing (NLP) model based on large language models (LLMs) that fine-tuned an LLM to retrieve and rearrange erroneous concepts in mathematical problems, facilitating automated matching of these concepts.

Utilized GPT-4o to generate supplementary training data to address the scarcity of annotated data. This step was crucial for enhancing the model's ability to understand and process mathematical language effectively.

Extracted key features from the mathematical problem text to construct training samples. This process ensured that the model learned from relevant data points that reflected common issues in mathematical problem-solving.

Employed a contrastive learning strategy based on the Qwen-14b/1932 model for fine-tuning. This approach helped the model differentiate between correct and incorrect concepts effectively. Implemented 4-bit quantization and LoRA (Low-Rank Adaptation) techniques to optimize model parameters, improving training efficiency and generalization ability.

In the retrieval and rearrangement stage, merged the embeddings of three heterogeneous LLMs for candidate retrieval. This multi-model approach enhanced the robustness of the retrieval process. Input the rearrangement statement into Qwen-32b-instruct-AWQ, utilizing its zero-shot capability to output new sorting statements. The top 25 results were processed in batches multiple times to refine the outputs. Simultaneously used bge-large-en-v1.5 to search the database using similarity measures to find the best results.
